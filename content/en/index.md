# LastRecall

LastRecall is a Terraria tModLoader mod. Players prepare weapons, ammunition, and supplies at a safe Base before entering dangerous Raid areas to explore, fight, and loot.

Finding something is not enough to keep it: only a successful extraction turns it into the result of the expedition. The most important question in a Raid is often not “Can I win one more fight?” but:

> **Should I keep looking for more, or extract now with what I have?**

![[raid.png]]

## Current development stage

**Early Development — the first complete playable Raid experience is being made.**

The main map in development is the **Desert Wasteland**. Current work is about making Base preparation, entering a Raid, exploration, combat, looting, deciding whether to go deeper, extraction, and returning to Base feel like one complete and natural session.

## A Raid at a glance

### Prepare

Choose firearms, ammunition, medical supplies, and other items to bring from Base.

### Deploy

Enter a separate dangerous area such as the Desert Wasteland.

### Explore and loot

Search buildings, containers, and different locations for supplies worth bringing home.

### Fight

Face enemies such as Scavengers; in multiplayer, other teams may also be encountered on different routes.

### Decide

The more supplies a player carries, the more the potential reward and the risk of dying both matter.

### Extract

Leave the Raid safely to truly bring recovered supplies back to Base.

### Go again

Sort, use, or trade the recovered loot, then prepare for the next trip.

## What makes LastRecall different?

### Looting and extraction

Finding valuable loot in a Raid does not mean it is yours yet. Going farther may find more supplies, but can also put everything already recovered at risk. Extraction is the moment that truly brings those gains home.

### Firearms and tactical information in Terraria

LastRecall has its own firearms, ammunition, and combat feedback. In a Raid, the direction a player is watching, walls, doors, windows, and sound all affect how much can be known about danger nearby. Seeing, listening, cover, and position all matter in a fight.

### Cooperation and competition in multiplayer

Multiplayer is not meant to turn every Raid into a deathmatch. On a busier server, players can form different teams and spread out to explore and loot, instead of everyone following the same path and competing for the same supplies.

Different teams may naturally meet because of their routes, buildings, loot, or extraction paths. Whether they avoid each other, watch each other, compete for resources, fight, or choose to cooperate is up to the players and the situation. A small group of friends can also enter as one team and play a Raid cooperatively.

### Base and the next trip

Recovered supplies can be sorted, used, or traded before choosing what to bring next time. Base and Raids are two halves of the same loop: one is for preparation, the other is for taking risks and bringing results home.
