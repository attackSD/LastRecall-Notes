# LastRecall Project Overview

LastRecall is a Terraria mod built around preparation, looting, and extraction. Players leave Base with chosen equipment, explore dangerous Raid areas, fight and search for supplies, then decide whether it is time to get out and bring those gains home.

It is not just about shooting enemies or opening chests. Every Raid asks questions: What should I bring? Is this loot worth the extra risk? Should I spend supplies to heal now, or save them for extraction? Those decisions give the same map a different story each time.

## The core idea

Equipment brought into a Raid creates risk. Items found there create temptation. Only extracting safely turns those temporary gains into resources that can help with the next trip.

Combat is part of that loop, not the only objective. Sometimes a player will go out of the way for one more container; sometimes leaving while already ahead is the better choice. LastRecall aims for readable, enjoyable Terraria adventures with meaningful decisions, rather than a harsh realism simulator.

## What players do

After choosing weapons, ammunition, and supplies at Base, players enter a Raid and move through open wasteland, buildings, and indoor spaces. They search containers, encounter enemies, and choose where to go next. In the current Desert Wasteland, areas such as the Gas Station Shop can become part of that route.

Back at Base, recovered supplies can be organized, used, or traded before the next expedition. Base and Raids are two halves of the same loop: one is for planning and sorting, while the other is for taking risks and bringing something back.

## Risk, death, and extraction

Going deeper can mean more loot, but also more encounters and more supplies spent. Extraction is not merely the exit door; it is the choice that turns a risky expedition into real progress.

Death interrupts a successful return and makes the equipment brought in and the loot found matter. Players can later return to a death location to try to recover dropped items, but that extra trip may bring its own risk.

## Base and Raids

Base is where players prepare, manage recovered items, trade, adjust equipment, and plan the next trip. A Raid is where they take risks, explore, fight, search, and try to return with something useful. They are meant to feel like two parts of one loop, rather than unrelated maps.

## Combat direction

LastRecall's firearms aim to feel clear, responsive, and weighty within Terraria. Muzzle flash and smoke, hit reactions, impact sounds, kill feedback, and different gunshot sounds in different spaces all help players understand what happened after a shot.

Rather than simulating every real-world firearm detail, the priority is readability: players should know that they fired, whether they hit, whether an enemy went down, whether the weapon is empty, and how close the danger is.

The current Raid build also limits exact hostile information through the direction a player is watching, real cover, and sound. This makes doorways, corners, and quiet rooms moments for watching, listening, and making a judgement rather than empty space.

## Multiplayer

Multiplayer is an important part of LastRecall. Players can enter Raids together, explore different spaces, cover one another, and decide together when to extract. Teammates are recognizable and do not damage one another in the current team flow. Seeing or hearing a teammate's fight can add another layer of judgement to a quiet search.

## Current development stage

The Desert Wasteland is the main Raid map. Current work is focused on making an early version of a complete play session feel natural: preparation, exploration, combat, looting, extraction, and returning to Base. Map detail, art, and content will continue to grow from there.

The M1911 is the most developed firearm so far. The AK-47 and MP5 can already be used through their basic flow, but still use temporary artwork. For a quick status snapshot, see [[en/00 - Project/Progress|Current Progress]].
