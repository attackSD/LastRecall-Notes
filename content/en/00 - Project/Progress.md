# LastRecall Current Progress

## In progress

**Desert Wasteland** is LastRecall's first major Raid. The shared normal-firearm foundation has reached a stable milestone, and the main focus is now building a graybox playable Raid vertical slice: prepare, enter, explore, fight, loot, move through the map, extract, and return to base.

## Current priorities

1. Build and test the playable Raid vertical slice in a Desert Wasteland graybox, from safe base preparation through extraction and return.
2. Lay out the Exterior, Gas-station Shop, spawn and combat spaces, indoor routes, loot and encounter locations, extraction route, and relevant sector transitions to test pacing, scale, spacing, and risk/reward.
3. Continue firearm content polish and pending checks—multiplayer muzzle effects, lethal-hit readability, final assets, audio, attachments, and balance—without making them the main project focus.

## Next directions

- Make enemy spawns and extraction points easier to tune for each map.
- Continue expanding the Gas-station Shop's scenes, exploration content, and art, and allow future maps to have multiple extraction points.
- Remake firearm and item assets; firearm modifications will also change weapon appearance.
