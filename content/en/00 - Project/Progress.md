# LastRecall Current Progress

## In progress

**Desert Wasteland** is LastRecall's first major Raid. The core vertical slice is playable, and the background Raid-sector lifecycle has passed Host & Play regression testing. Current work continues on map, exploration, enemy, and loot construction.

## Current priorities

1. Continue the M1911-versus-RaidScavenger gun-combat-feel slice, including remote multiplayer observation and lethal-hit readability testing.
2. Build Desert Wasteland's map, enemies, supplies, and atmosphere, and balance enemy strength.
3. Expand and balance M1911, attachments, crosshairs, firearm audio, Loot Box contents, and Recovery Broker prices.

## Next directions

- Make enemy spawns and extraction points easier to tune for each map.
- Continue expanding the Gas-station Shop's scenes, exploration content, and art, and allow future maps to have multiple extraction points.
- Remake firearm and item assets; firearm modifications will also change weapon appearance.
