# LastRecall Game Experience

A raid follows a straightforward loop:

1. **Prepare in the hideout:** Choose the equipment to take out.
2. **Travel to the wasteland:** Use the teleporter to enter Desert Wasteland.
3. **Explore and loot:** Search Loot Boxes, face enemies, and optionally visit the Gas-station Shop.
4. **Decide whether to extract:** Leaving safely matters more than carrying out more supplies.
5. **Return to the hideout:** Sort the haul and prepare for the next expedition.

## Current experience highlights

- Desert Wasteland is still an early map prototype; exploration routes and scenes are under construction.
- Loot Boxes reveal their contents gradually, while enemies create pressure during exploration.
- Extraction and the one-time post-death recovery system give every raid a clear risk.
- The Recovery Broker lets players sort and trade recovered supplies, though its inventory is still being balanced.

## Still needs polish

- Enemy strength does not yet scale with map difficulty; high-tier enemies still need damage and health tuning.
- The Gas-station Shop can be entered and exited normally, but its interior map has not begun construction.
- Several multiplayer flows still need verification; see [[en/30 - Release/Known Issues|Known Issues and Testing]].
