# LastRecall Game Experience

A Raid follows a straightforward loop:

1. **Prepare in the hideout:** Choose the equipment to take out.
2. **Travel to the wasteland:** Use the teleporter to enter Desert Wasteland.
3. **Explore and loot:** Search Loot Boxes, face enemies, and optionally visit the Gas-station Shop.
4. **Decide whether to extract:** Leaving safely matters more than carrying out more supplies.
5. **Return to the hideout:** Sort the haul and prepare for the next expedition.

## Current experience

- Raid entry, Exterior, sector travel, extraction, death return, and death-drop recovery are working.
- The Gas-station Shop now works as an independent indoor area; scenes, exploration, and art will continue to expand.
- Loot Boxes reveal their contents gradually, while enemies create pressure during exploration.
- The Recovery Broker can handle items brought back from raids, although loot and prices still need balancing.

## Still needs polish

- Desert Wasteland remains in an early map and content-building phase.
- Enemy strength, Loot Box contents, and trading prices still need adjustment.
- Firearm held poses, audio mixing, attachments, and the overall lineup will continue to expand.
