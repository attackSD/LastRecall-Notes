# LastRecall Game Experience

A Raid follows a straightforward loop:

1. **Prepare at Base:** Choose the equipment to take out.
2. **Go to Desert Wasteland:** Use the teleporter to depart.
3. **Explore and loot:** Search boxes, face enemies, and visit the Gas-station Shop.
4. **Choose when to extract:** Bringing supplies home is more important than pushing too far.
5. **Return to Base:** Sort the haul and prepare for the next trip.

## What can be played now

- Desert Wasteland, the Gas-station Shop, moving between areas, extraction, death return, and recovery of dropped items are in place.
- Loot Boxes reveal their contents gradually, while enemies create pressure during exploration.
- The merchant can handle items brought back from a Raid, although supplies and prices still need balancing.

## Gun combat feel

M1911 firing, hit feedback, and defeat feedback are gradually becoming one clear combat experience. Players can now see blood and a brief reaction on hits, hear a flesh-hit sound, and see muzzle flash and smoke. When firing close to a wall, the bullet and muzzle effects remain on the correct side of the wall. Other players seeing muzzle effects in multiplayer, and whether lethal hits are easy to notice in a normal fight, still need more testing.

## Still needs polish

- Desert Wasteland is still in an early map and content-building phase.
- Enemy strength, Loot Box contents, and trading prices still need adjustment.
- Held poses, audio mixing, different sounds for different spaces, attachments, and the wider firearm lineup will continue to expand.
