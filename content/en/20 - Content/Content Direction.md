# LastRecall Content Direction

## World

Desert Wasteland will be a desolate, dangerous location that rewards deeper exploration. The Gas-station Shop will become an indoor area connected to the wasteland, but its map and exploration content have not started yet.

## Firearms

The existing firearms, magazines, and ammunition have no obvious single-player functionality problems, but they rely heavily on external assets and audio, resulting in inconsistent quality.

A future firearm overhaul will use original assets and sound, and add a weapon-modification system. Modifications will change a weapon's appearance as well as its function.

## Items and supplies

The current item pool is small. Supplies and medical items have not been retested since the latest mechanics update. Future work will expand the content and use original assets.

## Loot and trading

Searchable Loot Boxes are already available and reveal their contents gradually. Search sounds are still missing and loot is not balanced. The Recovery Broker can buy and sell items normally; its inventory and prices are the next balancing target.

Unscheduled ideas are collected in [[en/20 - Content/Future Ideas|Future Ideas]] and kept separate from current development commitments.
